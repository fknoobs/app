This update is about the community: call people out in comments, rate the replays you watch, and start earning reputation for rewards later on.

## Mention players in comments

Type `@` in a comment and pick someone from the list. They show up as a mention you can click through to their profile — handy when you want to highlight a play or reply to a teammate.

![Typing @ in a comment opens a player mention picker](/whats-new/images/v0.61.0/mentions-2.png)

## Upvote or downvote replays

Found a great game? Give it an upvote. The score sits next to the replay title so the matches worth watching can rise to the top.

![Up and down vote buttons next to a replay title](/whats-new/images/v0.61.0/cast-a-vote.png)

## Reputation is live

Comments, votes, downloads, and matches you play now add reputation to your account.

> A later update will unlock rewards from that score. Keep contributing and you will be first in line.

## Watch replays on the website

You can now sign in on [coh1stats.com](https://coh1stats.com) with the same account as the desktop app, and open community replays in the browser.
